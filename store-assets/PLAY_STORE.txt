Short description:
Petteia, the Greek pincer game. Play against the computer or online, with the same board and style as the website.

Description:
ΠΕΤΤΕΙΑ is the board game of classical Greece. Move like a rook, capture by pincer, and win by leaving the opponent with no pieces or no moves.

• Guided tutorial
• Five difficulty levels, from Paidion to Palamedes
• Online games with a room code
• Spanish, English, French, and Russian
• The same marble, gold, and Aegean design as the website

Privacy policy: publish www/privacy.html on Vercel and paste the URL into Play Console.

When you turn advertising on:
1. Create the app in AdMob
2. Put the IDs in www/js/ads.js (enabled: true)
3. Uncomment AdMob in AndroidManifest.xml and android/app/build.gradle
4. Declare the AD_ID permission in Play Console
5. Upload a new AAB (versionCode 2)

The signing key is in android/keystore/ — KEEP IT. Without it there are no Play updates.
